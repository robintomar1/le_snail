# Le Snail

Le Snail concerns locomotion of an SO-101 arm while connected to external power and USB.

## Language

**Crawling**:
Repeatable forward movement of the arm through changing support against the ground, with the base generally underneath the arm. A crawl may be interrupted by a fall followed by recovery.
_Avoid_: Walking (when it implies conventional legs or a required stepping pattern).

**Recovery**:
The robot getting back into a posture from which it can continue crawling after a fall, without human assistance.
_Avoid_: Reset (which may involve repositioning the robot externally).

**Tether**:
The power and USB cables that remain connected to the robot during locomotion.
_Avoid_: Leash (which implies a restraint rather than these service connections).

**Tether drag**:
The resistance to the robot's motion caused by its connected tether.
_Avoid_: Floor friction (which also includes contact between the robot itself and the floor).
